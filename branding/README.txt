AK DIGITAL PRINTING – BRAND ASSETS
Source of truth: the supplied logo "Glossy_AK_Digital_Printing_Logo.png" (1774 x 887).

WHAT WAS DONE TO THE ORIGINAL
- Background removed (the source had a light checkerboard baked into the pixels, not real transparency).
  Outer background flood-filled from the edges; 3 enclosed background pockets under the gold swoosh removed by hand.
- Edges: colour-to-alpha on the 2 px outline band, so there is no white halo on dark backgrounds.
- One repair: the source had a checkerboard patch inside the "L" of DIGITAL (an artefact of the original file).
  It was painted with the same black as the surrounding outline. Nothing else in the design was changed:
  AK symbol, printing machine, CMYK ribbons, colours, proportions and the "DIGITAL PRINTING" text are original.
- Light sharpening only. The @2x file is an upscale for print layouts; it cannot add detail the original lacks.
- The logo contains no tagline and no service icons (as supplied).
